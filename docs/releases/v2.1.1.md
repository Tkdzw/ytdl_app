# YT Downloader v2.1.1

**Released 8 November 2026** · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · by Asher Naison

A fix release that brings back **4K and 1440p** YouTube downloads.

---

## Fixes
- **4K and 1440p downloads work again.** YouTube recently changed how it serves
  video, and many downloads were stuck at 360p. The app now lets yt-dlp choose how
  to talk to YouTube, so the full range up to 4K is available again.
- **You get the resolution you picked.** Choosing MP4 no longer quietly drops you
  to 1080p when a higher resolution exists. MP4 still prefers the most compatible
  video (H.264) whenever it's available at your chosen quality.

## Good to know
- YouTube only offers H.264 up to 1080p, so **MP4 above 1080p uses VP9 or AV1**.
  VLC and web browsers play these files; the Windows Media Player / Photos app
  needs the free *VP9 Video Extensions* from the Microsoft Store. Pick **1080p**
  if you need a file that plays everywhere.

## Installing
Download **`YTDownloader_Setup.exe`** below and install over your current
version. Your settings and download history are kept.
