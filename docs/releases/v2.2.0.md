# YT Downloader v2.2.0 “Eagle”

**Released 17 November 2026** · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · by Asher Naison

Codename **Eagle**. A speed release: each video downloads faster, and several videos can
download at once.

---

## What's new
- **Faster downloads, like a download manager.** Each video is now split into
  pieces that download over several connections at the same time and are put
  back together into one file. In testing this was about **2.5× faster**.
  Change the number under *Settings → Parallel connections* (1 turns it off).
- **Several videos at once.** New setting *Settings → Videos at once* (1–5,
  default 2). Each row shows its own progress, and the overall bar and time
  left take all of them into account.
- **Gentle on YouTube.** If YouTube says there are too many requests
  (HTTP 429), the app pauses, then carries on with fewer downloads and
  connections and speeds back up once things are calm. A video that hit the
  limit is retried automatically instead of failing.

## Fixes
- Stopping a download the moment it starts no longer leaves a partial file
  in your downloads folder.

## Good to know
- More is not always faster: around **2–3 videos at once with 4–8
  connections** works well on most connections. All downloads together use at
  most 16 connections.

## Installing
Download **`YTDownloader_Setup.exe`** below and install over your current
version. Your settings and download history are kept.
