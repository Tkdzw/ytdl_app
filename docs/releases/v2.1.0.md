# YT Downloader v2.1.0

**Released 8 November 2026** · Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) · by Asher Naison

This release makes **playlists** work properly. If you're coming from 1.x ("YT Batch Downloader"), it also
includes the whole 2.0 redesign — see [Upgrading from 1.x](#upgrading-from-1x).

---

## What's new in 2.1.0

### Playlists, one video at a time
- **Paste a playlist or channel link and every video gets its own row** — with its
  own title, length, progress bar, and Stop / Retry / Play buttons. Rows are
  labelled like *"Playlist: Holiday clips · 3 of 12"*, and each video is saved to
  your history separately.
- **Private and deleted videos are skipped automatically.**
- **Very long playlists ask first.** Anything over 150 videos shows how many there
  are before adding them.
- **"Just this video, or the whole playlist?"** When you paste a link to a video
  you opened from a playlist, the app asks which you meant. Tick *"Do the same for
  the other links like this"* and a big paste only asks once. YouTube Mixes are
  treated as single videos.
- **Press Start straight away.** If a playlist is still being read, downloading
  begins on its own as soon as the list is ready.

## Fixes
- **The progress bar no longer jumps back to 0 %.** A video and its sound used to
  fill the bar twice; they now count as one smooth 0 → 100 %. If a playlist can't
  be split into rows (some sites), you'll see *"Video 3 of 12"* and one steady bar.
- A finished download keeps the title it already showed.
- Thumbnails load a few at a time, so long playlists don't slow everything down.

## Upgrading from 1.x

Version 2.0 was a redesign for everyday use. Highlights:

- **New name:** YT Downloader (was "YT Batch Downloader").
- **Your downloads appear as a list on the home screen**, with Play, Open folder,
  Retry, Details and Remove for each one. The separate *Batch Queue* tab is gone.
- **Nothing extra to install.** FFmpeg and Deno are included. If you run the app
  without them, a *Finish setting up* window installs them for you in one click.
- **Simple choices:** *Download* Video (MP4) or Audio (MP3), and *Quality*
  Best / 1080p / 720p / 480p — 1080p MP4 by default, which plays almost anywhere.
- **Stop works instantly** and tidies up partial files; you can add links while a
  batch is running.
- **Clear error messages** that say what went wrong and what to do
  (e.g. *"This video is private."*), with the technical details one click away.
- **Overall progress** — *"3 of 10 done · about 5 min left"* — and a summary with
  Open folder / Play when everything finishes.
- **Easier to use:** simpler Settings with explanations on hover, readable text in
  light and dark mode (the theme follows Windows), and keyboard shortcuts —
  **Ctrl+V** adds links, **Ctrl+Enter** starts.

### Removed in 2.0
- The *Post-download Conversion* panel. Video is saved as MP4/MKV/WEBM and audio
  as MP3 directly, so it is no longer needed.

## Installing

1. Download **`YTDownloader_Setup.exe`** below and run it.
2. Upgrading? Just install over your current version:
   - Your **settings and download history are kept**.
   - Downloads still go to **`Downloads\YTBatchDL`** unless you changed it, so
     your old and new files stay together.
   - Old *"YT Batch Downloader"* shortcuts are removed and replaced.

## Good to know
- **Works with 1,600+ sites** via yt-dlp — YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Instagram and
  Facebook (public posts), X, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and many more.
- **Not supported:** DRM services (Netflix, Disney+, Spotify, etc.) and videos that
  need you to be signed in (age-restricted or members-only YouTube videos, private
  posts).
- Instant title/thumbnail previews are YouTube-only; other sites show their title
  once the download starts.
- Please only download content you have the right to download.
